// 01 — About

From turbulence theory to production solvers.

I'm an aerospace engineer and software developer. I earned my undergraduate degree in Aerospace Engineering at IIT Madras, then a Ph.D. at Cornell University in the Turbulence & Combustion Group, completed in December 2012.

For nearly a decade at Siemens I helped build the flow and combustion physics inside STAR-CCM+. Today, as a Principal Software Engineer at GE Aerospace, I lead the design and development of a high-order LES/CFD solver for hybrid CPU/GPU architectures — writing the code that simulates combustion inside jet engines.

// 04 — Research

Making combustion chemistry fast enough to be useful.

My work develops accurate and computationally efficient models and algorithms for simulating combustion. The methods were validated against experiment, delivered large speedups over existing approaches, and scaled across thousands of compute cores — now freely available and finding their way into commercial engine-design tools.
